PI Sarah Sebo Moves to the University of Kansas (KU)

As of summer 2026, PI Sarah Sebo has moved from UChicago to the University of Kansas (KU). From 2026-2028, the lab will be split across the UChicago and KU campuses with research activities continuing at both locations.

Prospective PhD Students: PI Sebo is eagerly looking to recruit 2-3 PhD students to enroll in the KU EECS PhD program for the upcoming recruitment cycle (applications submitted Fall 2026 to start Fall 2027). Follow the instructions here to apply.

March 18, 2026
🏆 Best Paper Award at HRI 2026 for Our Work on Robot Tutors and Child Social-Emotional Learning

Our paper Fictional vs. Factual Robot Tutor Dialogue Can Shape Child Social-Emotional Learning, led by Ph.D. student Lauren Wright and conducted in collaboration with Kiljoong Kim of Chapin Hall, received the Best Paper Award for User Studies at HRI 2026. This exciting work explores how the style of a robot tutor's dialogue — fictional versus factual — can shape children's social-emotional learning outcomes. The work has been featured in UChicago News, UChicago CS News, and Forbes. Congratulations to Lauren and all the authors!
